What the CSV contains and how it was derived (tools/cofid_to_csv.py):
  - sheet "1.3 Proximates" only; columns Food Code, Food Name, Energy (kcal) [KCALS],
    Protein (g) [PROT], Fat (g) [FAT], Carbohydrate (g) [CHO], AOAC fibre (g) [AOACFIB];
  - values are per 100 g of food as published; "Tr" (trace) is written as 0 and "N"
    (present in significant quantities but not reliably quantified) is written as empty;
  - a duplicated food code keeps its first row;
  - no value has been altered; Tiffin's ingredient catalogue (config/ingredients.yaml)
    references rows by food code and never edits them.
